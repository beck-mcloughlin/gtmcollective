Getting started

Simple on purpose.

You should know exactly what you're buying before you buy it.

  1. Intro call

    Thirty minutes on where you are and what's at stake. If it's not the right fit, you'll hear that, along with a referral to someone who is.

  2. Scoped proposal

    Within two business days: a fixed scope, a clear price and the finished product you'll have in hand.

    Kickoff within two weeks of signing. Retainers include a 30-day out clause, so you're never locked in.
